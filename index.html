Bisan Subba

Independent Multi-Platform Software Engineer • Solo Game Developer • 3D Generalist • Archviz & World Creator

Bisan Subba is an independent multi-platform software engineer and solo game developer focused on interactive digital experiences, cross-platform applications, and real-time 3D creation.

His work spans Unreal Engine development (C++/Blueprints), Electron desktop applications, web-based projects, environment art, and technical experimentation across multiple platforms including Windows, iOS, Android, Linux, and the web.

Alongside software development, he also creates 3D artwork and simulations using Blender, Plasticity, Substance Painter, Marmoset Toolbag, and Photoshop.

He is the developer of

Bsun Unit Converter (Microsoft Store), Bsun Calculator (Microsoft Store), Sky Gap Escape (App Store), Bsun Obstacle (Google Play), Bsun Nepali Date Converter (Microsoft Store), Sky Gap Escape Android (Google Play), Web-Based Table Tennis Game (Github pages ), and projects on itch.io.

Sky Gap Escape (App Store), Bsun Calculator (Microsoft Store), Bsun Obstacle (Google Play), and Bsun Obstacle Game is officially available on Linux, Android, Windows, and macOS (itch.io), and showcases his artwork on Pexels, reaching over 1.3M+ views and thousands of downloads.
